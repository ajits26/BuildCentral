About Rocketry

What is hobby rocketry?

Hobby rocketry is building and launching rockets that fly on commercially made motors, from small model rockets to high-power builds. It's a mix of engineering, craftsmanship, and spending a Saturday outdoors.

Getting started

Start with a beginner kit and a low-power motor. Build it, launch it at an approved field, and recover it. Most people move up to larger rockets once they're comfortable with the basics.

Join a club. Experienced members can review your build, lend tools, and show you what a good launch day looks like.

Safety basics

Launch only at approved sites, check the weather and wind first, and keep spectators well back. Use only commercially made motors and follow the safety code of the national organization you join (such as the NAR or Tripoli). Check their current rules before you fly, especially for high-power launches.
